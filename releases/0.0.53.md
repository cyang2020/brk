# Brk 0.0.53

- Swiping past your last Space now shows Arc's round + button and edge glow, in dark and light Spaces alike. The button takes its colours from the Space's theme, its ring fills as you pull, it pops when full, and it is gone the moment Create a Space opens.
- Tabs selected with Shift-click or ⌘-click can now be dragged together. They travel as a stack with a count, land together in sidebar order, and one ⌘Z puts them back. Drop them in the list, into a folder, on the Space name to pin them, on Favorites, on another Space in the bottom bar, or on another tab to make one split.
- Folders and tab groups, Tidy's included, show a soft rounded card around them while the pointer is inside, and the tabs in them are indented, as in Ardia.
- Place New Tabs now only suggests moving a new tab to another Space or into a group Tidy made, never into a pinned folder. The suggestion is a small arrow at the right of the tab: hover it to see where the tab would go, click it to move the tab. The "Move to …" line under the title, which was easy to click by mistake, is gone; right-click the tab to dismiss a suggestion. A suggestion also goes away once the tab moves on to another site.
- Fixed reordering a tab inside a Tidy group dropping it one place too low.
