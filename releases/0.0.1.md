# Brk 0.0.1

## Updates and release history

- Brk can download a signed update from the sidebar, then restart into the new version. You can also choose Brk > Check for Updates….
- Help > Release Notes now keeps a versioned history of useful changes. Each GitHub release keeps its own notes and downloads.
- If you use an older 0.1.0 copy, install this release from the disk image once. Those copies have no in-app updater. The new 0.0.1 label starts the versioned release history; increasing build numbers keep future updates in order.

## Downloads and appearance

- Active download cards keep their filled background and show a pale progress disc, including in dark themes.
- Websites respect their own dark color scheme without forced color inversion or an injected canvas background.
- The theme color picker now converts Display P3 correctly, keeping its saved and displayed colors consistent. You can drag the noise control from its center, undo changes, and adjust it with accessibility controls.
- Imported Space colors, gradient direction, theme transparency, and sidebar animations more closely follow Arc.

## Browser and installation fixes

- BrkCore embeds pages directly in Brk windows, removing the extra Chromium page-host windows that could remain behind them.
- Quitting no longer waits on background system media-permission checks.
- Disk-image packaging preserves the signed app and verifies its signature inside the finished image, fixing downloaded apps that failed strict verification.
- Site controls close when you click outside. Command-W closes the controls without closing the tab behind them.

## Earlier fixes

These fixes shipped in the builds before 0.0.1, newest first. 0.0.1 and every later version include them.

- **Some text became unreadable in dark mode.** Little Arc addresses, headings in site controls, dialog fields and keycaps, Library labels, AI conversation details, and Space names now stay readable against their backgrounds.
- **Onboarding tiles and badges were hard to read.** Favorites letter tiles keep enough contrast with white letters. The Recommended badge uses a lighter accent in dark mode.
- **Settings and menu commands did nothing after the last browser window closed.** Those commands now reopen a browser window and show their page or panel.
- **Reload stopped working after choosing Stay.** Refusing a reload now clears the pending reload, so the next Command-R works. Page dialogs identify the site instead of displaying the full address.
- **Context menus lost actions near the bottom of the screen.** Page menus and Library download menus now open above the pointer when there is not enough room below it.
- **Canceling Print or Save left a status notice on the page.** A cancellation that leaves no file now clears the notice. Successful saves show readable, centered text. Warnings about files that remain on disk stay visible.
- **Some Settings controls had no accessible button names.** Profile, app icon, and Air Traffic Control controls now expose named buttons. Disabled controls stay inactive.
- **Empty Brk windows appeared in Mission Control.** Chromium’s transparent page hosts were listed as separate windows. Brk now keeps them out of Mission Control and window cycling, including after leaving full screen.
- **Dark pages had a pale, rough rim around their rounded corners.** The white card fill and shadow backing showed through the edge pixels. They now stop inside the page edge, so the corner blends with the page and Space.
- **Four ways to crash Brk.** Importing an ad-block list with an empty option (such as `$domain=`); converting a very large time in the command bar; a history address longer than 64 KB, which crashed Brk again a few seconds after every launch; typing only "|" in Browse for Me. None of them crashes now.
- **Brk slowed down or locked up.** Browsing history was never trimmed and was saved in full on every change; the History and Media pages rebuilt every row and loaded every full-size picture on each key press; a page that kept showing alerts locked the whole app; a connected sync folder rewrote the whole state every minute. History now keeps a limited span, the pages load only what they show, and a page's alert stops only that page.
- **Tabs, history and settings could be lost.** A Live Folder refresh deleted tabs you had put in it; Delete on the History page could remove different visits from the ones checked; recording a shortcut picked up typing in browser windows and changed the shortcut; an AI message over 2,000 characters vanished with its text; Little Arc's Open in dropped its other tabs; undoing a Space delete flattened its folders; with Restore Windows on Launch off, tabs reopened at old addresses. All fixed.
- **Private browsing leaked into normal browsing.** Open Link in Incognito Window and Open in Little Brk from an incognito window opened the link in your normal profile; incognito favicons and command bar suggestions were stored in a shared disk cache and cookie store; a print that timed out left the page's PDF behind; deleting a profile left its Easels and AI conversations on disk. Private windows now keep nothing, and a deleted profile takes its files with it.
- **Features that did nothing.** Codex in a tab conversation refused every command without asking you (it now asks); changing the model in the middle of a conversation broke the next message; Stop on a first reply cut off Brk's tab tools; Print and Save Page As dialogs closed themselves after a minute; the Little Arc shortcut sent your typing to the other app; menu items chosen with the keyboard did nothing; Settings buttons acted on a random window; Hide cookie banners hid nothing; Open in Split View on a Favorite; Turn On Max; archiving Easel, History and AI page rows; Share in private and Little windows; Pinyin typed into the command bar being overwritten. All work now.
- **About 60 smaller fixes** to hover cards, Escape, Little Arc windows, closing the last window, find keeping its place when a page changes, auto-archive sparing tabs that play sound, Air Traffic Control route order, onboarding Back steps and more. One known issue is still open: closing a tab does not yet ask before throwing away text you typed into the page.
- **Scrolling the sidebar up and down, and swiping between Spaces, was still very slow with a large sidebar imported from Arc.** Brk kept a live view for every tab in a Space, including the hundreds out of sight, and macOS worked through all of them on every frame; a swipe also built the full lists of both neighbouring Spaces. The sidebar now keeps views only for the rows near what you see and adds the next ones as you scroll. With 5 Spaces and 560 imported tabs, Brk's main thread went from busy 98% of the time while scrolling and swiping to 40%.
- **The sidebar lagged when scrolling, worse with many tabs, folders and Spaces.** Every scroll step, key press and click made Brk recompute the whole window and redraw it, dozens of times a second, and an imported Arc sidebar made each pass slower. Brk now skips that when nothing changed: with 60 tabs, Brk's main thread is busy about half as much while scrolling the sidebar.
- **In full screen, the top-left corner was off.** With the menu bar shown, the strip under it started a few points in from the screen's edge, the window buttons were grey circles instead of red, yellow and green, and the sidebar button stayed where it leaves room for those buttons. The strip now starts at the edge, the buttons have their colours, and the sidebar button moves to the left as in Arc.
- **Spaces imported from Arc had the wrong colours.** A dark navy Space came out pale grey-lavender: Brk read Arc's colour slider backwards and ignored dark mode. Imported Spaces now keep Arc's colours in light and dark mode, and Spaces imported earlier are corrected the next time Brk opens.
- **Hover cards were hard to read in dark mode.** The Gmail "Recent Emails" card and the other Favorite, Pinned and folder cards turned dark in dark mode but kept black text, so they looked dimmed and stuck under the page. Their text and icons now turn light on the dark card.
- **The Today line did not move while Tidy worked.** Arc turns the line above Today into a moving wave while Tidy Tabs sorts the tabs; Brk's stayed straight. It now waves the way Arc's does, and the broom swings when you press Tidy.
- **Some text in Settings was invisible in dark mode.** The Previews sheet's title and checkbox labels, and the Air Traffic Control sheet's text, were white on a white sheet. They are dark now.
- **Dropping a tab group onto a group inside it deleted its tabs.** With a group inside a folder inside another group, dropping the outer group onto the inner one removed every tab in them without archiving them. Brk now refuses that drop.
- **A site's permission prompt could stay on screen with buttons that did nothing.** After Escape, a click elsewhere, or the page moving on, the prompt was given up but its card stayed. It now closes.
- **Brk kept busy in the background.** With more than eight ad-block lists in use (for example three profiles with three lists each), Brk reloaded them over and over and kept waking hidden tabs, so they never went to sleep. After a failed tab-naming request, it checked again ten times a second for five minutes. Both now wait as they should.
- **Importing some Arc sidebars never finished.** A sidebar file that lists an item twice, or inside itself, made the import build it billions of times. Each item is now read once.
- **Damaged data could make Brk loop or quit.** A kept AI page whose saved schedule was out of range refreshed nonstop, and a malformed reply from a local model made Brk quit. Both are now treated as invalid.
- **Brk quit when the pointer moved onto a row of a folder's hover card.** Hovering a collapsed folder, or a Favorite or Pinned tab that has a preview card, opens a card listing its pages. Putting the pointer on any row of that card closed Brk: highlighting the row redrew it, and the redraw asked for the highlight again, without end. Now the pointer on a row only highlights it, and ↑ and ↓ still move the highlight.
- **"Save password?" sat in the top-right corner of the page.** Brk now asks the way Arc does, with a card in the middle of the window: “Save password for (site)?” with Never on this site, No and Save. Saving an address or a card asks the same way (“Save Address?”, “Save this card?”).
- **Clicking an autofill suggestion did nothing.** Saved emails, addresses and passwords appeared in the list under a field, but neither a click nor ↓ and Return filled them in. The cause was a bug in the Chromium Embedded Framework 154 that Brk used; Brk now runs CEF 156 (Chromium 156.0.8078.88), where it is fixed.
- **Brk quit when a site offered to sign in with a passkey from your phone,** for example Canva's sign-in: Continue another way, then a passkey, then “Use a phone or tablet”. macOS closed Brk because Brk had not said why it might use Bluetooth. Brk now asks for Bluetooth permission instead, and declares every other permission a website can ask for.
