# Brk 0.0.54

- The "New Brk Version Available" pill now sits in its own panel at the bottom of the sidebar, as in Arc. Rest the pointer on it and Arc's card opens upward from it: the title, Automatic Updates and Restart and Update, in Arc's light or dark look to match your Space. The tab list ends above the card instead of showing around and behind it, and the card closes as soon as the pointer leaves. The release notes link stays, as a quiet line under the button.
- The tip about Today tabs being archived automatically sits in the same bottom panel, so tabs no longer scroll behind it.
