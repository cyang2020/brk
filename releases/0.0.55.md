# Brk 0.0.55

- Closing a page now lets the website finish saving first, the way Chrome and Arc do. Discord, and other sites that save your sign-in or drafts as you leave, no longer ask you to sign in again after a pinned tab is closed and reopened, or after Brk quits.
- If a page with unsaved changes is on screen, closing it, closing its window or quitting Brk now asks "Leave this page?". Stay keeps it open; Escape means Stay.
- Fixed Brk quitting when a site's own "Sign in with Google" (or other FedCM) button was clicked. The account chooser now opens over the page.
- Fixed Brk quitting when a site asked to connect to a USB, HID, serial or Bluetooth device. The device picker now opens at the top left of the page.
- Fixed Brk quitting when Chromium tried to show one of its autofill offers, such as protecting a saved card after filling it in. Brk keeps offering the saves it supports with its own cards.
